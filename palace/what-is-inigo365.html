what is inigo365?

inigo365 is a project i started towards the end of 2025- with no idea of what it was going to become. i just knew that for the first time i felt ready to embark on an intentional pursuit of establishing my artistic self. what became of it was something simple- a website i coined a living portfolio; one that ebbs & flows as my artistic practice around it grows. yet there was one simple thing standing in my way- the code.

all of a sudden it dawned on me- the barrier had eroded. if you couldn't tell already- a lot of this website was coded using ai. i'm no computer programmer. i'm an artist. so before you jump to any conclusions- let me explain myself.

for me to learn how to code to the level required to execute all of my creative ideas would take years. so i set myself a simple but strict rule- every creative aspect of this website, all images, music, videos, 3d models & all other creative decisions must always come from, and be made by myself. the only thing i use the ai for is for the technical element- the piece that otherwise would've taken an actual software engineer in the past.

i understand that by using a product that is directly putting money into the hands of the tech oligarchs- i am living within a contradiction. however, without using this tool- i would not have been able to pull all of these different visual, sonic & creative distinctions together into one cohesive website that i think accurately reflects how i feel about my place within the world. at a certain point, i wish to understand the tides of what i think is inevitably coming to our changing world over the next few years, as i think without understanding how the tools work- it's hard for me to feel like i can protect myself against the challenges that they provide.

but don't get me wrong- i'm extremely worried about ai. i'm extremely worried that the most powerful people in society have wealth that is growing at a rate exponentially faster than ever before in human history, and that the gap between the wealthiest & the poorest people in society is growing at a rate faster than ever before. it's terrifying, all while the world around us becomes increasingly more unstable, misinformation seeps into every aspect of society & distrust in our governments has become a direct response to this that is fracturing the political centre. if you aren't worried- you're not paying attention.

that fear and tension is very real, and to make matters worse- this uncertainty is being capitalised upon by the wealthiest in our society. if you can't make sense of why so many people are willing to vote for a reform government here in the uk for example, ask the average person how they feel about the cost of living. about paying for their groceries. about paying for their bills. about going out for an eight pound pint after work or seeing their favourite local restaurant closing down. there's no doubt that life is becoming increasingly more expensive, and that it's only within the last 50-60 years that our parents' generation were able to work simple jobs & afford a house. now, buying a house requires you to be either extremely lucky, or born into wealth.

so how does the average person make sense of this? i remember sitting in the truck at work one day with an older turkish ex-gangster turned truck driver, and getting into a heated debate about politics. as he regurgitated right wing talking points about immigration & public policy- i couldn't help but think that the root cause of his frustration was much less nuanced. that the things he brought up he seemed to be truly worried about- like not being able to be there for his children financially, or being able to spend enough time with his parents before they passed- these were the true causes of so much of his frustration. these things that felt universally relatable. experiences of love, loss & hardship that everyone goes through. yet at its core, his inability to read through the lines as a man who had never been properly educated (dropping out of school for streetlife at a young age) had left him more confused & angry than ever before. it didn't surprise me then, when my factual approach to the conversation left me with more questions than answers. no wonder he couldn't make sense of the world as it was laid out before him.

so it made me start to wonder what my purpose is in it all. i had always wanted to be an artist, yet something about making it started to bother me. as if, without purpose- the lack of intention in what i was creating found me feeling empty & hollow. so i decided to commit. inigo- my real name, 365 days a year. an intentional decision to be more like myself than i have ever been. to walk with purpose, and to articulate my understanding of the human experience with the hopes of being a vessel for those who cannot. i think that's what it means to be an artist really- to make things that help us make sense of the world.

therefore, i also believe that art is political by nature- existing as a reflection of our human experience. that "experience" is political in a sense that we are part of a global construct that forces us to participate through a system we call "democracy". it's our way of understanding the human experience & making sense of it. it's our way of understanding what it means to be human, and through understanding your own view of the world- you become a vessel through which others can do the same. no matter what the tech oligarchs want- ai cannot, and will not replace the human experience.

ai can't walk down to the shops and buy a rubicon mango, or talk to the homeless guy outside the shop about the england game. it can't be late for work, and it can't truly understand what it feels like to be living paycheck to paycheck. ai doesn't truly understand what it feels like to fall in love. these are deeply human experiences, and ones that make the art worth making.
