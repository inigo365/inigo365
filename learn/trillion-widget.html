Module 03 — The Contrast  ·  Actions / Harm
While accumulating wealth at a rate no individual in history has matched, Musk has used his platform — X, formerly Twitter, with 200M+ followers — to incite, amplify, and legitimise political violence internationally. A selection:
4 August 2024 — UK-wide riots
As far-right mobs attacked mosques and hotels housing asylum seekers across England, Musk posted directly to his 190M+ followers:
"Civil war is inevitable." @elonmusk, 4 August 2024 — viewed over 100 million times
UK Prime Minister Keir Starmer publicly criticised him by name. No correction or retraction was issued. The post remains live.
January 2025 — UK grooming gangs inquiry
During a politically charged debate about the grooming gangs inquiry, Musk targeted the sitting Prime Minister directly:
"Starmer is a rape gangs enabler." @elonmusk — posted repeatedly across multiple days in January 2025
"Is the UK Prime Minister complicit in the rape of Britain?" @elonmusk, January 2025
The posts amplified far-right disinformation at scale and were directly cited by those planning further unrest. Cabinet ministers described them as "dangerous interference" in domestic politics.
January 2025 — German federal election
Musk openly campaigned for the AfD — a party whose leaders have described the Nazi SS as "not all bad" and called for the end of Germany's post-war democratic consensus — and hosted their leader in a live X Spaces broadcast:
"Only the AfD can save Germany." @elonmusk, January 2025 — ahead of the German federal election
Multiple EU governments opened formal investigations. The European Commission described the interference as "unprecedented." The AfD came second in the subsequent election.
June 2025 — Belfast, Northern Ireland
During rioting in Belfast, Musk amplified far-right accounts and posted directly calling for the UK government to fall — including posts targeting Anas Sarwar, the Scottish Labour leader, with content that police described as incitement.
"The British government must fall. They are traitors to their own people." @elonmusk, June 2025
Community organisations and journalists on the ground attributed the accelerated spread of disinformation directly to X's algorithmic amplification under his ownership. Police made dozens of arrests.
2025 — United States, DOGE
As unofficial head of the "Department of Government Efficiency," Musk oversaw the dismantling of USAID, the CDC's global health programmes, and hundreds of federal programmes — with no elected mandate and little legal authority:
"USAID is a criminal organisation. Time for it to die." @elonmusk, February 2025
Cuts to USAID's food, medicine, and disease-response programmes are estimated by aid organisations to result in hundreds of thousands of preventable deaths globally. Many cuts were later ruled unlawful by US federal courts.
Ongoing — X / Twitter
Since acquiring Twitter in 2022 for $44B, Musk has reinstated thousands of banned far-right accounts, gutted trust and safety teams by over 80%, and restructured the algorithm to preferentially boost his own posts.
Hate speech, antisemitism, and harassment metrics on the platform increased sharply in the year following the acquisition, per multiple independent studies including from the EU Digital Services Act monitoring body. Advertisers fled. Users stayed.
This is a person with a trillion dollars, no democratic accountability, ownership of a global communications platform, significant influence over a sitting US administration, and a stated interest in shaping the political future of multiple countries he does not live in.
