Ash is an Amazon original directed by Flying Lotus. I worked as Art Department Runner, responsible for sourcing all materials on a tight budget and timeline. As the shoot progressed I moved across departments, working in set dec, greens, props and on-set props, and directly contributing to building and dressing the sets.

I led the majority of the final wrap, organising and clearing all remaining props and set decoration at the close of production.
