I worked in the Lighting department as a spark on One Last Shot, assisting the Gaffer across the full duration of production. My role involved rigging and striking Aputure, Arri, Astera and Dedo fixtures using a range of rigging solutions, as well as powering rigs from generators and distribution boards across different locations and set configurations. A demanding role requiring specialist knowledge and close attention to detail.
